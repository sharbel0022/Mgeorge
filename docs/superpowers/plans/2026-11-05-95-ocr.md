## Review focus

1. A failed or stale worker acknowledgement must not count as completed work or discard previously accepted work.
2. A replacement lease, rejected source, original change or company revocation must stop stale writes.
3. English totals/table labels and continued pages must not leak summary amounts into invoice lines or hide missing lines.
4. Ambiguous amounts, contradictory identities and VAT mismatch must stay review-required despite high OCR confidence.
5. Queue metrics must be read-only, owner-scoped, nonduplicating for repeated sources, and honest about timestamps and observation windows.
